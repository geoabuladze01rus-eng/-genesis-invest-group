PHASE I · COST PLAN MATURITY

Budget certainty grows with evidence.

Одна цифра CAPEX без указания maturity misleading. GENESIS разделяет conceptual estimate, preliminary cost plan, tendered cost и approved baseline.

L0 · CONCEPT

Benchmarks, unit rates, broad allowances and high contingency. Suitable for thesis only.

L1 · PRELIMINARY

Area schedule, product specification, site assumptions, utilities concept and package-level estimate.

L2 · TENDERED

Issued scopes, bidder clarifications, bid leveling, exclusions and market-tested package pricing.

L3 · BASELINE

Approved contracts, committed cost, approved contingency, change authority and forecast-at-completion.

CONTINGENCY

Contingency should shrink as uncertainty is retired.

Геология, slope stabilization, utilities, logistics, panoramic glazing, pools and amenity core требуют отдельных allowances до подтверждения. Переход к funding baseline возможен только после документированного reconciliation estimate → tender → award.
