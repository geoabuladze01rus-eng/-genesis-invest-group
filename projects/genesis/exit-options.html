PROJECT · EXIT / REPAYMENT

Exit logic is designed before capital enters.

Проектная экономика и доходность конкретного инвестора — разные вещи. Repayment / exit зависит от инструмента, cash waterfall, seniority, timing and definitive legal documents.

PROJECT CASH FLOW

Debt service or distributions from permitted project cash after operating costs, taxes, reserves and senior claims.

PHASE REFINANCING

Refinancing may be considered after de-risking if market terms, covenants and liquidity support it.

ASSET / SPV SALE

Sale of project, stake or stabilized asset can be an exit route if legally and commercially feasible.

SPONSOR / STRATEGIC BUYOUT

Buyback or secondary transfer only if expressly documented and financially supported; never assumed as guaranteed.

PROFIT DISTRIBUTION

Equity distributions follow the agreed waterfall, reserves and corporate approvals.

HYBRID CONVERSION

Convertible or hybrid structures require specific trigger, valuation and legal mechanics.

DOWNSIDE

Exit is not a promise of liquidity.

Investor materials should show base, delayed and downside routes, including what happens if sales slow, refinancing is unavailable or asset value declines. Final investor return is calculated only for the actual instrument and dated cash flows.
