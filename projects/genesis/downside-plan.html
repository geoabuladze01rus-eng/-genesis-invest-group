PHASE I · DOWNSIDE PLAN

Downside is managed before it becomes distress.

План реагирования связывает measurable triggers с заранее определёнными действиями. Цель — сохранить ликвидность, качество актива и возможность принять рациональное решение о следующей очереди.

SLOWER SALES

Reduce build pace, protect cash, reforecast absorption, review channels and product mix before broad discounting.

LOWER PRICE

Test mix, specification, payment schedule and phase size before accepting structural margin erosion.

CAPEX INFLATION

Re-tender, value engineer, split packages, freeze non-critical scope and consume contingency only through approval.

UTILITY DELAY

Re-sequence works, delay dependent commitments and update opening assumptions.

WEATHER / SLOPE

Protect site, shift critical activities, preserve drainage and reassess schedule contingency.

CONTRACTOR FAILURE

Step-in plan, replacement shortlist, package separation, material control and claims documentation.

LIQUIDITY PRESSURE

Stop discretionary commitments, prioritize critical obligations and activate funding-gate review.

QUALITY FAILURE

Stop replication, NCR/root-cause review, corrective mock-up and independent verification where warranted.

ESCALATION

Bad news should move faster than capital.

Material deviation escalates through project controls and Investment Committee. A next tranche or next phase is not automatic if downside assumptions breach approved thresholds.
