PHASE I · IC PACK

До движения капитала — единый пакет доказательств.

Investment Committee рассматривает не презентацию отдельно, а согласованный комплект commercial, technical, legal, financial и risk evidence с зафиксированными исключениями.

01 · INVESTMENT MEMO

Thesis, asset, sponsor, structure, ask, returns logic, downside, recommendation.

02 · FINANCIAL MODEL

Phase I quarterly cash flow, sources/uses, taxes, financing, sensitivity and liquidity.

03 · SITE DD

Title, access, restrictions, topo, geology, seismic basis and development feasibility.

04 · TECHNICAL DD

Masterplan, product, engineering, infrastructure, schedule, design maturity and constructability.

05 · MARKET

Comparable evidence, product-market fit, pricing, absorption and sales validation.

06 · COST / TENDERS

Cost plan, quantities, contractor prequalification, tender evidence, contingency and VE.

07 · LEGAL / TAX

SPV, investor instrument, sales model, security, permits, material contracts and tax memo.

08 · RISK REGISTER

Critical risks, owners, mitigations, due dates, residual risk and requested exceptions.

09 · FUNDING PLAN

Tranches, conditions precedent, minimum liquidity and follow-on funding logic.

10 · EXIT / REPAYMENT

Waterfall, repayment sources, investor exit mechanics and downside recovery routes.

DECISION RECORD

Approve · Conditional · Rework · Reject.

Решение должно фиксировать условия, лимиты, reserved matters, required evidence, ответственных и срок следующего review. Существенное отклонение после approval возвращается на committee/change-control.
