Reporting Standard ↗
PHASE I · INVESTOR REPORTING

No surprises.

Investor reporting должен показывать не только достижения, но и отклонения, причины, impact on cash/schedule, corrective action и решения, требующие согласования.

MONTHLY · CASH

Opening cash, inflows, payments, commitments, forecast and minimum-liquidity headroom.

MONTHLY · COST

Budget, committed cost, actual, approved changes, contingency and forecast-at-completion.

MONTHLY · SCHEDULE

Critical path, milestone status, procurement, permits, weather impact and recovery actions.

MONTHLY · SALES

Leads, visits, reservations, contracts, cash-in, cancellations, discounts and product mix.

MONTHLY · RISK

Top risks, triggers, owners, mitigations, overdue actions and new material events.

MONTHLY · QUALITY

Hold points, NCR, defects, rework, commissioning and reference-house lessons.

QUARTERLY · VALUE

Updated base/downside case, liquidity runway, phase-gate readiness and value bridge.

DECISIONS

Reserved matters, exceptions, new commitments and approvals captured in decision log.

EVIDENCE

Every material statement should point to a source.

Закрытый investor pack должен ссылаться на supporting documents: bank statements, contracts, site photos, progress certificates, tender comparisons, sales reports, permits and updated financial model versions.
