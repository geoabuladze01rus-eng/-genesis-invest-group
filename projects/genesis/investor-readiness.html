INVESTOR READINESS

Readiness is evidence, not presentation polish.

Этот dashboard показывает, какие блоки должны перейти из концептуального статуса в verified до institutional fundraising. Он не подменяет фактический due diligence.

LAND · REQUIRED

Выбранная площадка, title DD, restrictions, access, development feasibility.

TECHNICAL · REQUIRED

Topo, geology, seismic basis, utilities, infrastructure concept, design basis.

COMMERCIAL · VALIDATE

Comparable evidence, pricing ladder, reservations/pre-sales evidence, absorption.

CAPEX · TENDER

Phase I cost plan, tender returns, infrastructure quantities, contingency.

CASH FLOW · REQUIRED

Quarterly Phase I cash flow, sources/uses, runway, downside funding requirement.

LEGAL · REQUIRED

SPV, investor instrument, corporate rights, security, sales model, tax memo.

PERMITS · TRACK

Permitting matrix with evidence, owner, dependencies and critical path.

OPERATIONS · DEFINE

Management company scope, OPEX, service charges, rental-management economics.

FUNDRAISING RULE

No institutional “ready” label before the critical evidence exists.

До закрытия обязательных блоков материалы могут использоваться для предварительных переговоров, site sourcing и soft-circling капитала, но не должны создавать впечатление завершённого bankable investment case.
