Roadmap ↗
PHASE I · CONSTRUCTION SEQUENCING

Build the learning loop into the schedule.

Первая очередь должна создавать не только готовые дома, но и подтверждённую производственную систему для следующих 120 резиденций.

01 · ENABLE

Access, temporary works, survey control, drainage protection and construction logistics.

02 · INFRA CORE

Primary roads, power, water, wastewater and stormwater sections required for first cluster.

03 · REFERENCE HOUSE

Mock-up / reference residence validates details, interfaces, finishes and commissioning sequence.

04 · FIRST CLUSTER

Limited batch to verify actual productivity, defects, logistics and unit cost before scale.

05 · SERIAL WAVES

Repeatable clusters with frozen standards and measured learning curve.

06 · AMENITY CORE

Club / wellness / common landscape synchronized to sales and operating readiness.

07 · HANDOVER WAVES

Commissioning, snagging, document packs and owner handover in controlled batches.

08 · LESSONS LEARNED

Actual cost, duration, defects and buyer feedback feed the next phase gate.

STOP RULE

Serial defects stop serial replication.

Если reference house или first cluster выявляет системную ошибку, тиражирование узла останавливается до root-cause review, corrective action и approval нового standard detail.
