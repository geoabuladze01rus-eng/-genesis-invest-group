LAND

Title, restrictions, access, planning and transaction readiness.

TECHNICAL

Topo, geology, seismic design basis, utilities and buildability.

MARKET

Pricing evidence, product mix, demand, reservations and absorption.

COST

Estimate maturity, tender coverage, contingency and committed cost.

SCHEDULE

Critical path, permits, procurement, seasonality and commissioning sequence.

LIQUIDITY

Quarterly cash flow, minimum cash, downside runway and funding availability.

LEGAL

SPV, sales model, investor instrument, contracts and security architecture.

QUALITY / OPS

Reference house, QA system, handover, management company and service model.
